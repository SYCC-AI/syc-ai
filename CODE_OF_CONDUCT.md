# Code of Conduct

SYC-AI follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the work.
Harassment, personal attacks and sharing other people's private information are not
accepted in issues, discussions, pull requests or any other project space.

Report conduct problems privately to **syc@syc-ai.com**. Reports are handled
confidentially, and maintainers may remove content or block accounts that break
these rules.
