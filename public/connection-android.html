Android Connect your phone so the panel’s agents can act on it

1 · Install SYC Claw

SYC Claw connects one phone to this panel. It does nothing else — the agents in your professional accounts act on the phone through it.

2 · Sign in on the phone

Open SYC Claw, enter this panel’s address and your own username and password, then turn on access for SYC Claw when Android asks. The phone appears below once it is connected.
