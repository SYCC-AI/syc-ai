Guide

Welcome to SYC-AI

SYC-AI runs your own AI subscriptions — Claude, Codex and more — on your own computer, and lets you use them from the web, your phone or your desktop. This page shows everything you need, step by step.

Start in three steps

  1. 1 · Connect your computer

    Open “Professional monthly accounts”. Copy the one-line command for Linux / macOS or for Windows and run it on your computer, then sign in with your SYC-AI username and password. Node.js is installed for you if it is missing.

  2. 2 · Install and sign in Claude or Codex

    On the Claude or Codex card press “Install”, then “Sign in”. The sign-in page of Anthropic or OpenAI opens in your browser. Your login stays on your computer; SYC-AI never sees your password.

  3. 3 · Open SYC-AI — All in One

    Write what you need. SYC-AI sends each message to the engine that suits it, in one project folder with one shared memory, and shows who answered under every answer.

Professional accounts

These are your own monthly subscriptions — for example Claude Pro or Max for Claude, and ChatGPT Plus or Pro for Codex. SYC-AI does not sell them; it connects the ones you already have.

  • Add an account: on its card press “Install”, then “Sign in”.
  • A second account (for example personal and work): once the first one is signed in, the card shows “Second account” — sign it in there.
  • Choose which account each engine uses in SYC-AI → Settings → Engines → Accounts. SYC-AI never switches accounts on its own.
  • Gemini, Cursor and Kimi already install on your computer; signing in to them is on the way.

Automatic switching in SYC-AI All in One

Planning and writing go to Claude, building and fixing go to Codex, and short questions go to a lighter model. You can also pick the engine yourself for any message.

When one subscription reaches its usage limit, the same message continues with the next engine in your order, with a short summary of what happened, so your work does not stop. The usage of every account, with its reset time, is on the right side of the page.

Change the order, the models and the rules in Settings → Engines.

Skills and plugins

A skill teaches the agent a way of working — for example “write the test first” or “answer short and exact”. Skills make answers better and use fewer tokens.

  • Add one: SYC-AI → Settings → Skills and tools → Add. It is put into your project folder with your next message.
  • The token saver is on from the start. Every skill shows its author and licence.
  • Plugins (connectors) such as GitHub, Google Drive, Gmail and Notion will use each provider’s own official sign-in; they are on the way.

Specialized sessions

The yellow “+” opens sessions trained for one job: website builder, bug fixer, code reviewer, research assistant, writer and translator, data analyst, beginner coach and game maker. Start one with a click, or download it to use in any terminal agent.

Your phone

Open “Connect your phone”, install the SYC-AI app, sign in on the phone and tap “Connect this phone”. Then turn on “Agent alerts”: your phone tells you when an agent waits for your OK or finishes a long task. Nothing opens by itself.

Questions

Is it free?

SYC-AI (Main) is free until 31 December 2026. Plus, Pro and Immortal Edition open later; their price is shown before you choose anything.

Where do my files and logins stay?

On your own computer. Only the conversation passes through SYC-AI, so you can continue it on another device.

Does SYC-AI get around the limits of my subscriptions?

No. Each engine runs under your own account and its own limits. When one reaches its limit, SYC-AI can continue with a different engine you also have.

What may the agents do on my computer?

You choose in Settings → Permissions: read only, work in the project, or full access. SYC Node runs only the AI programs and works inside its own folder.

How do I install a plugin or a skill?

Skills: SYC-AI → Settings → Skills and tools → Add. Plugins with official sign-in are on the way and will appear in the same place.

How do I pause or remove it?

On your computer run “syc-node pause” to stop the panel from using it, “syc-node resume” to allow it again, or “syc-node uninstall” to remove everything.

How do I get help?

Open your profile → Support → New ticket, and choose technical, financial, or suggestions and criticism. You can also write to syc@syc-ai.com.
